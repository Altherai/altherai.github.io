Media Kit / Brand Fit

For Brands

ALTHERAI is a small gaming creator focused on practical PC gaming content: Warframe, free-to-play games, Linux/Zorin compatibility tests, challenge videos, Shorts and budget creator workflow.

  • Warframe players and returning players.
  • Free-to-play PC gamers looking for new games.

  • Small creators interested in free recording/editing workflows.

Content pillars

Any key, product, affiliate link or sponsorship will be clearly disclosed in the video, description or pinned comment. ALTHERAI does not promise fake positive reviews, hidden sponsorships, artificial engagement or guaranteed sales.
